**5. A freeform notes field on a queue item.**

**OVERRULED 2026-08-30 BY THE OPERATOR, for the VOICE NOTE only, and the incident this item was
written against is paid for rather than waived.**

His ask, in his own words: *"i want to override and say voice is needed to make it easy for users
to get through inbox"*. Recorded against row `0432`, his ask 3, and folded into
`outputs/2026-08-29-commander/05-PROJECT-PLAN.md` section 1A on 2026-08-31.

**What this item's incident actually is, which is what decides the scope of the overrule.** The
prohibition is not "no text near a queue item". Its own sentence is *"there is no box whose text
goes nowhere"*: every text box on a queue item is bound to one verb and one required argument, so
that nothing a person types can be swallowed. A freeform notes field breaks that because it is a
place to put words that reach no transition, which is a parallel list wearing the queue's clothes.

**So the condition is structural, and it is three things:**

1. **The transcript posts through the registered `note` verb, onto a NAMED row.** Not a new verb,
   not a new door, not a new table. `note` is verb 22 in `engine/VERB-PARITY.md` and is what every
   agent in this system already calls to say something about a task.
2. **There is still no box.** No textarea, no typed note, no freeform field anywhere on a queue
   card. The only thing that reaches `note` is a transcript. `test_queue_has_no_freeform_note_box`
   asserts that absence at the surface, because the allowlist line makes the verb reachable and a
   rendering change is now the only thing standing between here and the field this item forbids.
3. **The text comes from somewhere and says where.** A voice note is a counted row in
   `brain.voice_capture` before the microphone opens (migration 25), and the note carries the
   transcript in full plus the retained audio's path and sha256. Nothing is summarised and nothing
   is fabricated: a capture whose engine heard nothing lands a note that says so and names the
   audio, because `null` and `unavailable` are refused a body by the table's own CHECK.

**The overrule is ONE LINE on the allowlist and it is not a widening of the room.** `voice open`,
`voice recorded`, `voice retained`, `voice transcribed` and `voice failed` are all registered verbs
and **none of them is reachable from any room**. The capture runs as the `voice` CLI in a
subprocess, writing as itself exactly as it does from a terminal, and the console posts only the
finished transcript. A rendering surface has no business being able to open a microphone and still
cannot.

**What stays prohibited, unchanged:** a typed freeform note, a comments field, any box whose text
does not reach a verb, and `note` in any other room.

*Checked:* `test_queue_cannot_set` (the rest of the original assertion, with `note` removed and the
reason recorded), `test_queue_has_no_freeform_note_box` (the condition above, at the surface), and
`test_the_voice_note_lands_on_the_row` (the transcript reaching the thread through the verb, with
